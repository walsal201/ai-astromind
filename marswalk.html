Rapid temperature cycling: surface warms from −80°C dawn to −20°C midday peak, then rapid cooling. EVA suit thermal regulation load increases dramatically in afternoon. Schedule primary EVA 08:00–14:00 LMST. Avoid EVA at night (radiative cooling 100 W/m² from suit). Battery drain rate increases 23% below −50°C.
Source: REMS temperature data (Curiosity) · InSight HP3 thermal measurements
