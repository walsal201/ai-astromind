The Mars Challenge
The first astronauts on Mars need the best map possible
NASA has explored Mars robotically for decades — collecting topography, mineralogy, atmospheric, and terrain data. But none of that data has ever been unified into a single tool designed for a human explorer on the surface, in a suit, making real-time decisions about where to walk, what to sample, and when to turn back.
