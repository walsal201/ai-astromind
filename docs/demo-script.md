# AstroMind — 3-Minute Demo Video Script

## VIDEO STRUCTURE (180 seconds)

---

### [0:00–0:25] HOOK & PROBLEM (25 sec)

**Visuals**: Space imagery, mission control room footage, complex mission planning spreadsheets

**Narration**:
> "Deep space mission planning requires thousands of specialist-hours and millions in consulting fees. Small research teams and emerging space agencies simply don't have access to these tools. What if AI could democratise expert-level mission planning?"

---

### [0:25–0:55] SOLUTION INTRO (30 sec)

**Visuals**: AstroMind prototype opening, header animation

**Narration**:
> "Introducing AstroMind — an AI-powered deep space mission planner built on IBM watsonx, developed entirely using IBM Bob as the primary development tool."

**On screen**: Show the 5-tab interface. Highlight the IBM watsonx badge.

---

### [0:55–1:35] LIVE DEMO (40 sec)

**Step 1** (10 sec): Click "Run in Demo Mode". Select Mars, Crewed Mission, 2027, 4 crew.
> "In seconds, AstroMind generates a complete mission profile — Δv budget, launch windows, transit calculations, life support margins — all powered by IBM Granite."

**Step 2** (15 sec): Click "Analyze Mission Risks". Show risk cards populating.
> "The risk matrix automatically identifies critical failure modes with mitigation strategies — radiation exposure, EDL uncertainty, communication blackouts."

**Step 3** (15 sec): Go to AI Chat tab. Ask: "What is the optimal launch window for Mars in 2027?"
> "And teams can ask any mission question in plain English and get expert-level answers instantly."

---

### [1:35–2:10] ARCHITECTURE (35 sec)

**Visuals**: Architecture diagram from README

**Narration**:
> "AstroMind uses IBM watsonx Granite-13b-Instruct as its reasoning engine. A domain-grounded prompt layer injects orbital mechanics constants to prevent hallucination. The entire prototype runs in the browser — no server required."

**On screen**: Show the trajectory visualiser with Hohmann transfer arc.

---

### [2:10–2:40] IMPACT & IBM BOB (30 sec)

**Narration**:
> "IBM Bob was used throughout — from architecture design and prompt engineering to code generation and documentation. This project demonstrates how IBM's AI tools can accelerate real engineering work."

**On screen**: Show mission brief being generated, then the GitHub README.

> "AstroMind reduces preliminary mission planning from weeks to minutes, making deep space exploration more accessible than ever before."

---

### [2:40–3:00] CLOSING (20 sec)

**Visuals**: Space, rocket launch

**Narration**:
> "AstroMind — because the future of space exploration belongs to everyone."

**On screen**: `github.com/walsal201/Intelligent-Systems-for-the-Future-of-Work` · IBM watsonx logo · IBM Bob logo

## KEY DEMO TALKING POINTS

1. **IBM Bob as primary tool** — not just used for code snippets, but for architecture, prompts, and docs
2. **watsonx Granite model** — enterprise-grade, STEM-optimised, responsible AI
3. **Domain grounding** — how injecting orbital constants prevents AI hallucination (unique technical contribution)
4. **Demo mode** — works without API key, judges can try it immediately
5. **Real-world impact** — specifically quantify: weeks → minutes for mission planning

## RECORDING TIPS

- Record at 1920×1080
- Use OBS Studio or Loom
- Keep mouse movements slow and deliberate
- Zoom into the AI typing animation — it's visually compelling
- Narrate while interacting — don't let silence linger
- Upload to YouTube (unlisted) or Vimeo for public link
